Frequently asked

How do I delete my account?

In the app: Profile tab → Settings (gear, top right) → Delete my account. You'll confirm twice, then your profile, photos, matches and messages are permanently erased. If you can't access the app, email us and we'll delete your data for you.

What is "the Veil"?

Sisters can keep their photos softly frosted until they choose to unveil them for a match. It's entirely her choice, and she can browse and chat while staying veiled. Nobody can remove another person's veil.

How do I report or block someone?

Open their profile and tap the … menu in the top right, or use the menu inside any chat. You can report, block, or unmatch. Reports go to our moderation team. Blocking is immediate and mutual — you won't see each other again.

What is a wali / chaperone?

You can invite a guardian to observe a conversation, in keeping with Islamic courtship. It's optional, you control it, and a wali never gains access to your account.

Is Veiled free?

Yes. Veiled is free to use, and Veiled Gold is free during launch — there are no charges in the current version of the app.

Who can join?

Veiled is for adults aged 18 and over who are seeking marriage.

I found a bug, or something isn't working

Please email us with your device model and iOS version and we'll look into it.

Safety

  • Never send money to anyone you meet on Veiled.
  • Meet in a public place, and tell someone you trust where you're going.
  • Report anything that feels wrong — we read every report.
