veiled — Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026 · Applies to the Veiled iOS and Android apps ("Veiled", "we", "us").

Veiled is a marriage-matchmaking app built around modesty and privacy. This policy explains what information the app handles, and the choices you control.

1. Information stored on your device

Veiled is designed offline-first. The following is stored locally on your device and is not transmitted to us unless online features are enabled (see section 2):

  • Your profile (name, age, city, occupation, bio, veil style, interests, values, deen preferences, marriage timeline)
  • Photos you add to your profile
  • Your matches, likes, messages, reactions and event RSVPs
  • App preferences (filters, theme, notification settings)

Deleting the app deletes this local data.

2. Information we collect when online features are enabled

When you create an account on Veiled's online service, we collect and store:

  • Email address — to create and secure your account
  • Profile content — the profile you write, including your veil style and photo-privacy ("Veil") settings
  • Photos — served only according to your Veil settings: if your photos are veiled, they are not shared with other members until you choose to unveil them for a specific match
  • Messages and voice notes — delivered to your matches; also visible to a wali/chaperone only if you invite one to a conversation
  • Friend's Take content — notes submitted by people you invite to vouch for you
  • Push notification token — if you enable notifications
The Veil, in plain words: photos you mark as veiled are frosted for everyone. They are revealed only to a match you personally choose to unveil them for. This is enforced on our servers, not just in the app.

3. What we do NOT do

  • No advertising, and no sale or sharing of your data with data brokers or advertisers
  • No tracking across other companies' apps or websites
  • No collection of your device's precise location
  • Selfie verification runs to confirm you are a real person; your verification selfie is never shown on your profile

4. Camera, microphone and photos permissions

The app asks for camera/photo access only when you add profile photos or verify your profile, and microphone access only when you record a voice message or use voice matchmaking. You can use the app without granting these permissions.

5. Data retention and deletion

You can delete your account at any time in the app (Settings → Account). Deleting your account permanently removes your profile, photos, matches and messages from our servers. Local data is removed by deleting the app.

6. Age

Veiled is for adults seeking marriage. You must be 18 or older to use the app.

7. Security

Account passwords are stored hashed (bcrypt); sessions use signed tokens; traffic to our servers uses HTTPS. No system is perfectly secure, but we design for data minimisation: the less we hold, the less can go wrong.

8. Changes

If this policy changes materially, we will update this page and the "Last updated" date, and notify you in the app where appropriate.
